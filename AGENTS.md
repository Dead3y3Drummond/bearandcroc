# Bear & Croc project instructions

Before changing visual design, read `BRAND.md`, `brand/README.md` and `PROJECT_WORKFLOW.md`.

Use the appropriate Bear & Croc family: `website` for website pages and simple intake forms; `app` for interactive tools and working applications. Furnace Trolls is an app-family game variant with its own established art and type. A form inside an app inherits that app's family.

Preserve the existing family, typography, approved identity and interaction conventions unless the user explicitly requests a change. Do not reinterpret brand consistency as requiring every product to use the website's warm palette. An existing simple intake does not become an app merely because it has validation, steps or conditional fields.

Use the recorded colors and font stacks. Do not invent font identities from logo or generated artwork, bundle unlicensed fonts, or silently substitute a new family. Record intentional changes in the brand reference and project handoff.

The shared tokens are a reference until deliberately integrated. This repository's instructions do not automatically propagate to other repositories; follow the adoption procedure in `brand/README.md`.

The October 7, 2026 adoption is reference-only. Do not change live properties, runtime styles or assets merely to adopt the standard. Such changes require a separately authorized task.
